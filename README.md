## 🌍 About Phucuka Mzansi Foundation

The **Phucuka Mzansi Foundation** is dedicated to uplifting communities across South Africa through hands-on technology education, digital skills development, mentorship, and youth empowerment initiatives. By teaching real-world software development and modern technical tools, we bridge the digital gap and create pathways toward sustainable employment, entrepreneurship, and long-term social mobility.

---

## 🎯 Core Focus Areas

* **Technical Skills Training:** Practical, cohort-based instruction in full-stack web development, modern tools, and software fundamentals.
* **Youth Mentorship & Career Readiness:** Preparing emerging talent with industry-standard project workflows, code reviews, and production portfolios.
* **Tech-for-Good Platforms:** Designing and deploying open digital solutions and community-focused systems to solve operational challenges.
* **Strategic Partnerships:** Collaborating with grassroots youth organizations, educational institutions, and corporate sponsors across South Africa.

---

## 🛠️ Organization Workspaces

* **`pmf-operations`:** Internal operations tracking, board milestones, partner outreach, and community roadmaps.
* **Curriculum & Labs:** Practical student exercises, technical roadmaps, and code challenges for PMF development cohorts.
* **Digital Projects:** Open-source tooling and community initiatives managed by the foundation.

---

## 🤝 Partner & Collaborate With Us

We welcome collaborations with corporate partners, volunteer technical mentors, and fellow non-profit organizations:

* **Sponsor a Cohort:** Fund digital infrastructure, student equipment, and internet access for training cohorts.
* **Technical Mentorship:** Lead guest workshops, code reviews, or career development sessions for PMF students.
* **Community Initiatives:** Work with us to deploy digital solutions for local community organizations.
