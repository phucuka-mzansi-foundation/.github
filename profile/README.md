## 🌍 About Phucuka Mzansi Foundation

The **Phucuka Mzansi Foundation** is a non-profit technology academy and human capital accelerator based in South Africa. We uplift youth and underserved communities through practical software engineering education, digital literacy, and community-driven technology initiatives. By combining modern production engineering stacks with registered national qualifications, we build degree-alternative pathways into high-value technical careers.

---

## 🏛️ Statutory Accreditation & Regulatory Alignment

PMF delivers standardized technical training aligned with South Africa's **Occupational Qualifications Sub-Framework (OQSF)** governed by the **Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO)**:

| Metric / Parameter | National Registration Details |
| :--- | :--- |
| **Qualification Title** | Occupational Certificate: Software Developer |
| **SAQA Qualification ID** | **118707** |
| **Curriculum Code** | 251201-005-00-00 |
| **NQF Level** | Level 5 |
| **Total Credits** | 220 Credits (~2,200 Notional Hours) |
| **Accreditation Authority** | Quality Council for Trades and Occupations (QCTO) |
| **Assessment Quality Partner (AQP)** | MICT SETA (National EISA Administration) |

## 🎯 Core Focus Areas

* **Full-Stack Engineering & Data Systems:** Hands-on labs covering HTML5/modern web frameworks, Python development, SQL/NoSQL databases, MVC patterns, and Git version control.
* **Youth Mentorship & Work Readiness:** Connecting emerging developers with industry mentors, capstone projects, and real-world software delivery workflows.
* **Community Digital Solutions:** Developing open, production-ready software to address local operational challenges across South Africa.
* **Corporate Learnership Pipelines:** Partnering with tech enterprises to facilitate workplace experience components (`WE-01` to `WE-04`) and graduate placements.

---

## 🤝 Enterprise Capital & Corporate Partnerships

Partnering with PMF unlocks measurable statutory and fiscal benefits for corporate sponsors and employers:

* **B-BBEE ICT Sector Scorecard:** 100% of training expenditure and learner stipends claimable toward **Statement AICT300** (6% payroll target, 20 scorecard points).
* **Section 12H Tax Allowances:** Up to **R80,000** in statutory tax deductions per learner (**R40,000** commencement + **R40,000** completion), scaling to **R120,000** for candidates living with documented disabilities.
* **SETA Discretionary Grants:** Facilitation of MICT SETA co-funded learnership cohorts covering institutional tuition and candidate stipends.
